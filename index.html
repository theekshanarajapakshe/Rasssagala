01 / THE MOUNTAIN

Know the peak

Not Rajagala (Ampara). Not the Ratnapura Rassagala. This is Raxagala / Kinihira Kanda — second-highest of the Dolosbage range, a flat-topped hill above the tea estates with its own patch of jungle.

WHAT IT IS

RAXAGALA RAKSHAGALA KINIHIRA KANDA PATTINI AMMA KANDA WANGEDI MOLGAS GALA

Second-highest peak of the Dolosbage range, 1,438 m. Named for its mortar-and-pestle shape (Wangedi Molgas Gala). Flat summit carries its own isolated patch of jungle — a good overnight camping spot.

Trailhead is the Raxawa junction on the Nawalapitiya–Dolosbage road, ~14 km from Nawalapitiya. The trail starts past the Raxagala tea factory. Two paths from Raxawa: a clearer one along the tea estate, and a slightly harder one through tea-farm land.

Distance: 2–3 km one way, ~260–370 m gain depending on tracker. 1.5–2 hr for light hikers; budget 2.5–3 hr with 13 boys and camping gear. Rated moderate; the final climb one blogger called "a bit difficult".

RAKSHAGALA — ROCK OF THE RAKSHASAS

Folklore links the mountain to Ravana: the "Rock of the Rakshasas", said to have hidden Sita. A gold bed is supposedly hidden somewhere on the mountain. The flat summit's isolated patch of jungle is the campsite — the boys sleep where the old story sleeps.
